Privacy Policy for Anbar

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Delegate Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game Anbar (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Anbar.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Game and the services enabled in the Game, we and our service providers may collect or process the following types of information:

  • Device and technical information, such as device type, operating system, app version, and similar technical information.
  • Advertising identifiers and information used for advertising and measurement.
  • Gameplay and usage information, such as interactions with the Game, session information, and performance information.
  • Diagnostic and crash information used to maintain and improve the Game.
  • Account information, such as your in-game username, player identifier, authentication credentials and session, handled through Unity Authentication.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the Game unless specifically required for a feature and disclosed to you.

2. Unity Services

Anbar uses services provided by Unity Technologies, including Unity Authentication, Unity Analytics, and other Unity Gaming Services.

These services may process information such as device information, gameplay activity, identifiers, authentication information, and diagnostic information in order to provide functionality, analytics, security, and service improvement.

For more information about Unity's privacy practices, please visit Unity's Privacy Policy .

3. Advertising

Anbar uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements, including interstitial ads before a match.

Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses (which can be used to estimate general location), device and account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. This information is used for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Ad personalization depends on applicable law, consent and your privacy settings.

Where required, the Game requests your advertising consent using Google's consent interface before requesting ads. When applicable, you can revisit these choices using Ad privacy options in the main menu. You can also manage your advertising identifier in Android settings.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Analytics

We use Unity Analytics to understand how players use Anbar and to improve the Game.

Analytics information may include gameplay events, session information, device information, performance information, and other usage information.

5. Authentication and Account Information

Anbar uses Unity Authentication to provide account-related functionality. When you use authentication features, Unity may process information associated with your account and authentication session.

We use this information to provide features such as identifying your account, maintaining game progress, and providing online functionality.

6. Cloud Services

Anbar may use Unity Gaming Services, including Cloud Code and Cloud Save, to provide online game functionality and store game-related information.

Information stored through these services may include game progress, player data, settings, and other information required to provide the Game's functionality.

Online multiplayer and team voice chat

Anbar uses Photon Fusion for online matches and Photon Voice for teammate voice communication. Player identifiers, display names, team assignments, gameplay state and connection information are processed to provide multiplayer sessions. Your display name and team are visible to other players in a match.

If you grant microphone permission and leave your microphone unmuted, microphone audio is transmitted through Photon Voice to teammates in your voice channel. You can mute your microphone using the in-game control or revoke microphone permission in device settings. Receiving other players' voice can be muted separately. For information about Photon, see Photon's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Google Play processes real-money purchases. Anbar processes purchase information, such as product identifiers, order information and purchase tokens or receipts, to verify transactions, grant virtual items and prevent duplicate or fraudulent claims. We do not receive your full payment-card details from Google Play.

7. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate Anbar.
  • To authenticate players and provide account functionality.
  • To save and synchronize game progress.
  • To provide online and cloud-based functionality.
  • To provide advertisements.
  • To measure advertising performance.
  • To analyze gameplay and improve the Game.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized use.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Sharing of Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the Game, including Unity Technologies, Google and Photon (Exit Games), and their relevant partners.

These service providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements with us.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, our Game, or others.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Game, maintain accounts and game functionality, analyze and improve our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Retention periods may also depend on the policies of the third-party services used by the Game.

10. Children's Privacy

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us at the email address below.

You may also have controls provided by your device or operating system for advertising and privacy preferences.
